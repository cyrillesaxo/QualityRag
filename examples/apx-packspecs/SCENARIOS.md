# What the APX-derived cases represent

The user's original PackSpecs are **high-level governing specifications**, not simply the list-of-requirements metadata accepted by QualityRag v0.1. These 15 cases are executable **projections** of five source artifacts (four families, with FFI Teacher Transformation as a separate addendum). The fixture cannot verify the truth, independence or provenance of caller-supplied witness labels.

## SEMLOAN v0.4.0 — Learning convergence (uploaded YAML)

**Enterprise learning assistant:** A workforce training team deploys a 180-page onboarding course on identity authentication and records learner satisfaction, quizzes, notes, generated answers and next-week retrieval probes.

- **INCONCLUSIVE:** Learners report *“I understood it”*, but no learner-owned cold retrieval or transfer FieldOutcome is supplied. SEMLOAN calls felt fluency a deceptive witness. The v0.1 gate only reports the missing declared evidence category.
- **ADMIT (metadata only):** A learner-owned attempt is recorded and a cold transfer outcome is supplied. A full SEMLOAN evaluation must still check the **3+ day** retrieval boundary, AI-shortcut provenance, adaptive routing, analogy-ledger refinements, and governing version.
- **REFUSE:** A review establishes that the apparent learner-owned attempt used an AI shortcut; the witness marked `CONTRADICTS` blocks admission. No psychological assessment is implied.

Source-specific guards **not implemented**: R0_VACUUM confrontation prevention, content-gated refinement triggers, G_FOSSIL, G_UNANIMITY, target-version drift, or actual learning certification.

## Question Fan v0.1.0 — Governed missing questions (uploaded text)

**Enterprise research assistant:** An LLM receives 100 supplier agreements and recommends a migration. A potential question challenges the assumption that the supplier permits regional data transfer.

- **INCONCLUSIVE:** The assumption is registered, but the system has not established how resolving the question would change the decision.
- **ADMIT (metadata only):** Original objective, challenged assumption, and material consequence have supplied witness categories.
- **REFUSE:** Independent review contradicts the claimed decision impact.

Source-specific guards **not implemented**: 0–3 surfaced questions, rejecting generic questions based on content, preservation of exact original answer, or **user selection before investigation**. An admitted metadata receipt must never trigger an external action without user authorization.

## FFI v0.6.0 — Forensic corpus convergence (uploaded PDF)

**Investigative assistant:** A client asks whether a newly published enterprise security procedure is authoritative across several editions of scanned manuals, changelogs, and incident reports.

- **INCONCLUSIVE:** Verbatim text and a test are available, but corroboration from a distinct source is missing.
- **ADMIT (metadata only):** Custody, discriminating probe, and independent corroboration labels are provided. The source's governing constraint remains unverified by this SDK.
- **REFUSE:** A measured probe contradicts the proposed constraint.

Full FFI additionally requires WitnessGraph, custody and scope, five-stratum grammar, deception census, time/version validity, receipt and observability residue. The original describes itself as a release candidate **unvalidated** for its full research program.

## FFI Teacher Transformation v0.6.0 — Education (uploaded PDF)

**Educational RAG assistant:** A knowledge base combines hundreds of engineering tutorials, prerequisite diagrams, code samples and training materials, and generates a lesson intended to work equally for Spring Boot and .NET engineers.

- **INCONCLUSIVE:** The answer carries a minimal witness source, a prerequisite map and an atomic validation artifact, but lacks the **cross-framework back-translation probe** required by the source's agnostic-training standard.
- **ADMIT (metadata only):** W_min, prerequisite map, atomic test, and a reported Spring-to-.NET portability check are supplied as distinct evidence classes.
- **REFUSE:** The tested back-translation explicitly contradicts the portability claim.

Full FFI Phase T also calls for B4 pathway decomposition, E2 Engine-Action-Target normalization, the Baeldung mini-fact discipline, G9 prerequisite checks, and validation of dynamic metadata routing; these operations are not executed by the v0.1 gate.

## Software-selling PackSpec v0.1.0 — Verifiable buyer value (uploaded PDF)

**Software sales assistant:** A consultant analyzes procurement emails, incident reports, technical integration notes, and results of a prospective enterprise pilot.

- **INCONCLUSIVE:** The buyer has a documented recent operational failure and prerequisites have been checked, but no rival/baseline comparison supports the new system's claimed advantage.
- **ADMIT (metadata only):** Buyer-state, integration, and controlled-comparison evidence classes all appear.
- **REFUSE:** The actual controlled trial contradicts the claim of improvement.

Full PackSpec requirements include the **k0–k4 calibration ladder**, five grammar strata (PRIMITIVE, STATE, RULE, REGIME, DRIFT), DW/DS/DD screens, and W_min. A v0.1 QualityRag `ADMIT` is **not a k3 or k4 license** and cannot replace real independent measurements.

**Reference:** Cyrille Eloundou, [APX](https://apx.guide/). These are synthetic training scenarios, not field results.
