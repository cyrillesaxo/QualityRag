Retrieved 2026-11-04. This register makes no priority or superior-compression claim.

## What this implementation actually establishes

The current palette scene-state codec is an intentionally small predictive
reference: a shared backdrop palette, explicit frame order/rate and lossless RGB
residuals. It is not a learned semantic scene model and is not FF-RCT. Its value
is as a runnable comparator for preservation, local activation, reusable modules,
streaming, lifecycle measurement and falsifiable future codec research.

The system-level research question is whether externally reviewed, purpose-scoped
preservation contracts can select reusable representations with a measured
lifecycle advantage. That question remains open. No claim of being first, of
clinical correctness, of a useful neural codec advantage, or of autonomous novel
grammar invention follows from this implementation.

## Failure register

Retain the entire experiment receipt for: larger payloads than AV1, setup costs
that never amortize, unavailable energy/device measurements, incompatible
models, unknown applicability, withheld or absent independent reviews, and any
critical-field loss. Publish no savings estimate from missing costs. A paired
evolution arm that does not improve a qualified baseline is a negative result.
